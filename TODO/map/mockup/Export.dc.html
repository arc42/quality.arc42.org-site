## 10 Quality tree

- **#reliable**
  - **Availability** — The degree to which a system is operational
    and accessible when required for use.
  - **Fault tolerance** — The ability to continue operating as
    intended despite hardware or software faults.
  - **Recoverability** — The ability to recover data and re-establish
    the desired state after an interruption or failure.
  - **Resilience** — The ability to keep delivering an acceptable
    level of service while faults disturb normal operation.
- **#secure**
  - Resilience (also listed above)

### Own requirements

- **Checkout keeps working when the payment provider is down**
  (Availability, Resilience, Fault tolerance)
  When the payment provider returns errors for more than 30 seconds,
  checkout queues the order and tells the customer. No order is lost.
