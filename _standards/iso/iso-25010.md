## Product Quality Model Characteristics

| Characteristics | Description | Sub-Characteristics |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **[Functional Suitability](/qualities/functional-suitability)** | The degree to which a product or system provides functions that meet stated and implied needs when used under specified conditions. | [Functional Completeness](/qualities/functional-completeness) <br> [Functional Appropriateness](/qualities/functional-appropriateness) <br> [Functional Correctness](/qualities/functional-correctness) |
| **[Performance Efficiency](/qualities/performance-efficiency)** | The performance relative to the amount of resources used under stated conditions. | [Time Behavior](/qualities/time-behaviour) <br> [Capacity](/qualities/capacity) <br> [Resource Utilization](/qualities/resource-utilization) |
| **[Compatibility](/qualities/compatibility)** | The degree to which a product, system or component can exchange information with other products, systems or components, and/or perform its required functions, while sharing the same hardware or software environment. | [Co-Existence](/qualities/co-existence) <br> [Interoperability](/qualities/interoperability) |
| **[Interaction Capability](/qualities/interaction-capability)** | The degree to which a product or system can be operated by specified users to achieve specified goals with effectiveness, efficiency and satisfaction in a specified context of use. (Formerly Usability) | [Appropriateness Recognisability](/qualities/appropriateness-recognizability) <br> [Learnability](/qualities/learnability) <br> [Operability](/qualities/operability) <br> [User Error Protection](/qualities/user-error-protection) <br> [User Engagement](/qualities/user-engagement) <br> [Inclusivity](/qualities/inclusivity) <br> [User Assistance](/qualities/user-assistance) <br> [Self Descriptiveness](/qualities/self-descriptiveness) |
| **[Reliability](/qualities/reliability)** | The degree to which a system, product or component performs specified functions under specified conditions for a specified period of time. | [Faultlessness](/qualities/faultlessness) <br> [Fault Tolerance](/qualities/fault-tolerance) <br> [Availability](/qualities/availability) <br> [Recoverability](/qualities/recoverability) |
| **[Security](/qualities/security)** | The degree to which a product or system protects information and data so that persons or other products or systems have the degree of data access appropriate to their types and levels of authorization. | [Confidentiality](/qualities/confidentiality) <br> [Integrity](/qualities/integrity) <br> [Non-Repudiation](/qualities/non-repudiation) <br> [Accountability](/qualities/accountability) <br> [Authenticity](/qualities/authenticity) <br> [Resistance](/qualities/resistance) |
| **[Maintainability](/qualities/maintainability)** | The degree of effectiveness and efficiency with which a product or system can be modified by the intended maintainers. | [Modularity](/qualities/modularity) <br> [Reusability](/qualities/reusability) <br> [Analysability](/qualities/analysability) <br> [Modifiability](/qualities/modifiability) |
| **[Flexibility](/qualities/flexibility)** | The degree to which a product or system can be adapted for different or evolving hardware, software or other operational or usage environments. (Formerly Portability) | [Testability](/qualities/testability) <br> [Adaptability](/qualities/adaptability) <br> [Scalability](/qualities/scalability) <br> [Installability](/qualities/installability) <br> [Replaceability](/qualities/replaceability) |
| **[Safety](/qualities/safety)** | The degree to which a product or system avoids harm to people, business, software, property or the environment in a specified context of use. | [Operational Constraints](/qualities/operational-constraint) <br> [Risk Identification](/qualities/risk-identification) <br> [Fail Safe](/qualities/fail-safe) <br> [Hazard Warning](/qualities/hazard-warning) <br> [Safe Integration](/qualities/safe-integration) |
