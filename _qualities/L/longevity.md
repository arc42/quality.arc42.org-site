>This specifies the expected lifetime of the product.
>
>Example: The product shall be expected to operate within the defined maximum maintenance budget for a minimum of five years.
>
>[Volere+2012, p. 56](/references/#volere)

<hr class="with-no-margin"/>

Longevity is about how long a product is expected to stay in service and remain useful, typically in the face of changing business requirements and technology.
It is not about growth in data volume or load; for the expected increase in size a product must handle, see [scalability](/qualities/scalability) and [capacity](/qualities/capacity).
