Definition:

>extent to which behaviours or outcomes, or both, of a product meet (satisfy) specified quality requirements when used.
>
>[ISO/IEC 25019:2023](/references/#iso-25019), §3.2.1.3

Suitability is a **quality-in-use** characteristic. It asks whether the outcomes of using a product satisfy the quality requirements specified for it — whichever qualities those requirements happen to concern. A waterproof device is thereby suitable for underwater use, whatever functions it offers.

[Functional suitability](/qualities/functional-suitability) asks the narrower question of whether a product *provides the functions* that meet stated and implied needs. The two are defined in different SQuaRE standards — suitability in the quality-in-use model [ISO/IEC 25019](/standards/iso-25019), functional suitability in the product quality model [ISO/IEC 25010](/standards/iso-25010) — so they are not synonyms.

The [#suitable](/tag-suitable/) dimension takes the term in this broader sense.
